Academic homepageManaf Saidi

Manaf Saidi

Operations management · Decision analytics · Human–AI systems

My research interests lie at the intersection of operations and technology management, analytical decision-making, and complex systems. I am particularly interested in how organizations can make robust decisions under uncertainty, and how simulation and AI-based tools can support those decisions.

My background spans mechanical engineering, management research, and data analytics. I have worked on topics ranging from microfluidic systems and biomedical engineering to maritime supply chain resilience and digital twins. These experiences inform my interest in quantitative models and their practical use in organizational decision-making.

Research interests

Decision-making under disruption

Operational decisions in environments exposed to geopolitical risk, disruptions, and uncertain information, with particular interest in supply chain resilience and maritime logistics.

Digital twins and decision support

Simulation-based decision support, surrogate modeling, and the use of real-time information to assess alternative operational responses.

Human–AI collaboration

How people interact with AI systems in decision processes, including oversight, reliability, accountability, and the allocation of decision authority.

Additional research experience: microfluidic engineering, particularly continuous-flow size-based fractionation of tumor spheroids and organoid-scale samples.
